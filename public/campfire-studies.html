01

TREATMENT 01 / VERSION 1

The Living Forest

Cinematic magical realism

A believable woodland gathering, with natural textures, moonlight, and a warm circle of fire. The magic lives in the atmosphere.

The most photographic of the four.

What this could mean for the site

An immersive site with spacious dark sections, photographic scenes, and restrained gold accents.

02

TREATMENT 02 / VERSION 1

A Circle of Belonging

Luminous storybook painting

A warmer, softer gathering with flowing fabrics, expressive faces, and a forest that feels protective and alive.

Close to 01 in realism; its painterly shift is subtle.

What this could mean for the site

An inviting site with painterly imagery, softer transitions, and an emphasis on people and relationships.

03

TREATMENT 03 / VERSION 1

The Woodland Ceremony

Art Nouveau nocturne

Fine golden botanical contours, jewel-colored shadows, and a celestial sky turn the woodland into an illuminated scene.

More gold linework and decorative detail than 01 or 02.

What this could mean for the site

An ornate, mystical site with botanical details, gold rules, and rich illustrated passages.

Open original ↗
04

TREATMENT 04 / VERSION 1

The Common Fire

Graphic woodland folk art

Flat shapes, carved contours, and a limited gold, teal, and lavender palette. A hand-printed world with a strong graphic identity.

The clearly graphic, print-like treatment. This was the fourth image.

What this could mean for the site

A cohesive illustrated site where animals, trees, fireflies, and fire can become recurring visual motifs.
