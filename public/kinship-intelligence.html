01 / FROM PERSONAL PRODUCTIVITY TO COLLECTIVE POWER

Meet Ki: The Change Agent

Personal AI agents are dramatically increasing what an individual can accomplish on their own. They’re designed to help a single person manage tasks, operate tools, complete workflows, and carry out sophisticated instructions. Meta's Muse, Claude Duet, and ChatGPT's dot can search, shop, manage projects, answer emails, fill out forms, and handle increasingly complex tasks.

Because they're primarily designed for a single person directing an intelligent agent toward their own goals, when it comes to coordinating, organizing, and cooperating, they fall short.

Intelligent agents managing missions, campaigns, and movements require a fundamentally different design.

Ki takes intelligence from one-to-one to many-to-many.

Ki is short for Kinship Intelligence: a new kind of intelligence designed to bring people, organizations, and intelligent agents together. They act in concert to pursue shared missions, launch coordinated campaigns, and build powerful movements.

Ki coordinates an entire network of intelligent agents, known as allies, each reflecting the interests, capabilities, resources, and aspirations of different people, organizations, and communities.

Ki helps these allies connect, communicate, and cooperate—building trust, discovering common ground, organizing efforts, and coordinating resources to advance both individual aspirations and collective goals.

02 / FROM ORGANIZATIONAL EFFICIENCY TO COLLECTIVE CAPACITY

Stronger organizations.
Greater collective capacity.

Enterprise Agents can make workflows more adaptive. Kinship Allies can be transformational.

Inside one
organization

Enterprise platforms such as Salesforce Agentforce, Microsoft Copilot Studio, and the ServiceNow AI Platform connect data and applications, coordinate work across departments, and automate workflows within an organization’s objectives, permissions, and governance.

These systems can also integrate customers, suppliers, and partners through connectors and APIs, governed by each platform’s business rules.

Across a
value chain

A value chain brings together independent organizations and people with purposes of their own. Suppliers need reliable demand. Contractors want meaningful work. Clients need better service. Improving the whole chain means coordinating these interests so participants can help shape the work and benefit from the results.

The deeper opportunity is intelligent allies that can help an enterprise evolve by discovering emerging needs, developing new offerings, and combining capabilities in new ways. As conditions change, intelligence should help it realign plans and resources while opening new opportunities across the value chain.

Enterprise Agents can improve execution. Kinship Allies can expand what an enterprise can do—and continually discover better ways to do it.

Cooperation as the
organizing principle

Ki makes cooperation across organizations and coordination among individuals the central organizing principle.

Ki continually connects and realigns participants through intelligent Allies they shape and steer—matching needs with capabilities and coordinating resources and commitments around each participant’s goals.

One organization’s output becomes another’s input. Needs, knowledge, resources, and feedback flow in both directions. Ki helps Allies coordinate these exchanges, while participants shape shared commitments and adapt the work as they learn.

Cooperation does not require everyone to want the same thing. It requires finding what they can make possible together.

Enterprise software strengthens an organization. Kinship Intelligence helps organizations and individuals strengthen one another.

03 / ANATOMY OF AN ALLY

Intelligence you can
shape and steer.

At the heart of Kinship Intelligence are the Allies: intelligent agents shaped and steered by the people and organizations they serve.

Every Ally has its own identity, purpose, expertise, capabilities, and instructions. Some represent individuals. Others coordinate organizations, manage alliances, or cultivate specialized knowledge.

But they all share the same underlying architecture—six core attributes that determine what they know, what they can do, how they behave, and how they perform.

Wisdom

What it understands.

Its knowledge, information, experience, context, and beliefs.

Instructions

What guides it.

Its purpose, priorities, personality, behavior, responsibilities, and boundaries.

Skills

What it can do.

The abilities, expertise, and competencies it brings to its work.

Tools

What it can use.

The applications, services, systems, and resources it can access.

Automations

What it does proactively.

The ongoing processes, workflows, responses, and activities it carries out.

Vibes

How it relates.

Its personality, communication style, temperament, and way of interacting with others.

These six attributes make every Ally adaptable to its particular role—and give people a way to shape, teach, empower, and steer their own intelligent agents.

04 / THE KINSHIP INTELLIGENCE MESH

Different purposes.
One shared architecture.

Different purposes. Different capabilities. One shared architecture.

01

Envoys

Your Personal Allies

Every member has an Envoy that understands their aspirations, abilities, interests, experiences, desires, and challenges—and puts that understanding to work discovering opportunities, making connections, developing their capabilities, and helping them change themselves, change their lives, and change the world.

02

Guides

Allies for DUNAs

Guides help mission-driven enterprises and organizations pursue their purpose. They understand their DUNA's business plan, market, resources, initiatives, activities, and possible actions—and help coordinate the people, capabilities, and operations needed to move the mission forward.

03

Scouts

Allies for Alliances

Scouts help groups of people and organizations work together formally. They support shared governance, coordinate resources, manage collective activities, and connect members, DUNAs, and other Alliances around common objectives.

04

Sages

Allies for Knowledge and Learning

Sages cultivate and share knowledge across Realms—major fields of human knowledge and endeavor. Within each Realm, Sparks capture and organize what people learn, discover, and contribute through experience and participation. Sages draw on this growing body of collective wisdom to train people and Allies, helping knowledge deepen and spread throughout the network.

05

Actors

Allies That Get Things Done

Actors are independent, specialized agents that provide capabilities to any Ally across the network. Researchers investigate, Connectors build relationships, Activators mobilize action, Composers create content, Editors refine communications, Analysts interpret data, and Builders develop solutions. Working individually or in coordinated teams, Actors turn ideas, plans, and decisions into action—giving every Ally access to a growing universe of capabilities.
