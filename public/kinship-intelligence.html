01 / FROM PERSONAL PRODUCTIVITY TO COLLECTIVE POWER

Meet Ki: The Change Agent

Personal AI agents are dramatically increasing what an individual can accomplish on their own. They’re designed to help a single person manage tasks, operate tools, complete workflows, and carry out sophisticated instructions. Meta's Muse, Claude Duet, and ChatGPT's dot can search, shop, manage projects, answer emails, fill out forms, and handle increasingly complex tasks.

Because they're primarily designed for a single person directing an intelligent agent toward their own goals, when it comes to coordinating, organizing, and cooperating, they fall short.

Intelligent agents managing missions, campaigns, and movements require a fundamentally different design.

Ki takes intelligence from one-to-one to many-to-many.

Ki is short for Kinship Intelligence: a new kind of intelligence designed to bring people, organizations, and intelligent agents together. They act in concert to pursue shared missions, launch coordinated campaigns, and build powerful movements.

Ki coordinates an entire network of intelligent agents, known as allies, each reflecting the interests, capabilities, resources, and aspirations of different people, organizations, and communities.

Ki helps these allies connect, communicate, and cooperate—building trust, discovering common ground, organizing efforts, and coordinating resources to advance both individual aspirations and collective goals.

Personal agents extend the power of the individual. Kinship Intelligence multiplies that power through cooperation and coordination. The more people, organizations, and intelligent allies join forces, the more powerful each become—and the more we can accomplish together.
