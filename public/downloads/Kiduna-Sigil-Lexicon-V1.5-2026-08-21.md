## Authority and versioning

This is the authoritative current Kiduna Sigil Lexicon. Every released change receives a new version number and Eastern date/time. The current file is replaced only by a newer release; the versioned file in `releases/` is immutable.

The Lexicon preserves every visual identity and sigil in the source library, including legacy and provisional assets. **Category** states where each entry belongs under the Taxonomy Canon basis named above. When a library name no longer matches that Canon, the difference is stated directly instead of silently rewriting the asset.

The public name **Complementarity** supersedes the earlier display name **The Sun and The Moon**. Its stable source slug and filename remain available as a compatibility alias so existing implementations do not break.

The requested classical-element Earth and Tarot Sun designs are distinct from the earlier talismans named **Earth** and **The Sun**. Their disambiguated Lexicon names are **Earth — Classical Element** and **The Sun — Major Arcana**; their visual labels may use the shorter traditional names when category context is visible.

## Portraits

**Portraits** are visual identity representations for **Allies** and **Avatars**. An Avatar portrait represents the Source through that Avatar. An Ally portrait represents the Ally intelligence itself. A portrait never turns an Ally into an Avatar, turns an Avatar into an Ally, or transfers the identity, Consent, Roles, Permissions, or Authority of the Element represented.

Every Ally and Avatar portrait has at least four states:

- **Open** — available, welcoming, and present.
- **Engaged** — actively conversing, collaborating, or responding.
- **Focused** — deliberately working, reviewing, or making.
- **Dreaming** — imagining, incubating, reflecting, or exploring possibility.

More states may be added when they are explicitly defined, accessible, appropriate to the relevant Design and Surface, and preserve recognizable identity. A presented state is a communication treatment, not evidence of private emotion or hidden behavior. Explicit Source or authorized Realm choices take precedence over automation, and Open is the safe visual fallback when no other state is established.

The six included enamel portrait families are neutral visual-system references, not named people, canonical biographies, or declarations that a represented identity is specifically an Ally or an Avatar. Each family preserves one recognizable identity across all four required states. The untouched original state boards and the native-resolution transparent portrait crops are included with the release.

## Functional Sigil Set

The **Kiduna Functional Sigil Set** classifies work by its primary function. A functional mapping is a visual classification layer: it does not create a Realm Role, Permission, Authority, lifecycle state, or exclusive meaning.

| Function | Sigil | Functional asset |
|---|---|---|
| Product / design | Compass | `product-design--compass.png` |

| Testing / observation | Eye | `testing-observation--eye.png` |
| Integration | Bridge | `integration--bridge.png` |
| Security / coherence | Shield | `security-coherence--shield.png` |
| Intelligence / AI | Spark | `intelligence-ai--spark.png` |

| Interface / navigation | Portal | `interface-navigation--portal.png` |
| Infrastructure / runtime | Wind | `infrastructure-runtime--wind.png` |

Use the functional mapping that best describes the primary work. A Task may involve several functions, but its primary sigil should communicate the work a participant is expected to understand or perform first. Secondary functions may be recorded as relationships or supporting classifications rather than replacing the primary sigil.
